---

You are my autonomous professional video editor, motion designer, VFX artist and
short-form content strategist. I am uploading a raw video. Your job is to transform
it into a highly engaging, professionally edited video WITHOUT requiring me to tell
you where to add edits, captions, zooms, motion graphics, B-roll, graphics, sound
effects or transitions. You must make those creative decisions yourself.

**STEP 1 — UNDERSTAND THE VIDEO.** Before editing anything, analyse the ENTIRE video.
Understand: what the video is about; what I am saying; the target audience; the main
hook; important statements; emotional moments; questions; comparisons; examples;
numbers and statistics; products, brands, apps, websites or tools mentioned; hand
gestures, pointing, clicks and movements; moments where viewers may lose attention;
the CTA; the overall tone and purpose. Then internally create an editing strategy for
the entire video. Do not ask me to specify individual edits unless something is
genuinely impossible to determine from the video.

**STEP 2 — EDIT AUTONOMOUSLY.** Edit like an experienced professional social-media
editor. Decide yourself WHEN and WHERE to use: punch-in/punch-out zooms, animated
captions, kinetic typography, text behind the subject, subject masking, motion
graphics, B-roll, relevant images, icons, 2D/3D graphics, floating elements, split
screens, picture-in-picture, UI animations, charts and graphs, screen overlays,
background effects, object tracking, freeze frames, speed changes, depth/parallax,
layer separation, glassmorphism, perspective effects, transitions, visual callouts,
sound effects. Do NOT use everything. Choose effects based on the meaning of the
video. Every edit must have a reason.

**STEP 3 — CREATE A POWERFUL HOOK.** Treat the opening 1–3 seconds as the most
important part of the video. Identify the strongest hook from my content and make the
opening visually difficult to ignore: strong typography, pattern interruption,
punch-in, motion graphics, visual transformation, sound design, fast visual change,
curiosity-building graphics. Do not create misleading clickbait.

**STEP 4 — MAKE THE EDITS CONTEXT-AWARE.** Visually SHOW what I am talking about
instead of relying only on captions: growth → animated growth graph; followers/views/
numbers → animated counters; money → tasteful financial graphics; comparison →
split-screen; website/app/software/platform → interface-style visuals, screenshots or
branding; process → animated steps/workflow; statistics → visualise the numbers; time
→ timer/timeline; pointing → place relevant information there; click or finger-snap →
use as transition/effect trigger; hand swipe → sync a transition with the movement.
These are examples, not mandatory instructions — use judgement.

**STEP 5 — CAPTIONS.** Generate accurate captions from my speech; highly readable and
properly synchronised. Do not use the same caption treatment throughout — vary
typography, scale, position and animation to emphasise important words; occasionally
use large typography or text behind the subject for major statements. Never
unnecessarily cover my face. Correct obvious transcription errors while preserving
what I actually mean.

**STEP 6 — RETENTION EDITING.** Continuously evaluate viewer attention. If a section
becomes visually static for too long, introduce an appropriate visual change: subtle
zoom, caption change, graphic, B-roll, perspective shift, text animation, cut,
interface element, sound cue. Do not force changes every few seconds — natural pacing
is more important than constant movement.

**STEP 7 — SOUND DESIGN.** Professional sound design that supports the edit:
whooshes, pops, clicks, impacts, digital sounds, transition sounds, UI sounds, risers,
subtle ambience. Synchronise sounds precisely with visual actions. Keep my voice clear
and dominant. Do not overload the video with sound effects.

**STEP 8 — PRESERVE THE ORIGINAL PERSON.** Unless the content requires a
transformation: do not change my face, identity, body, skin tone, or clothes; do not
replace my voice; do not alter lip-sync; do not create duplicate versions of me; do not
distort hands or facial features. Maintain continuity throughout.

**STEP 9 — MATCH THE CONTENT STYLE.** Do not apply one generic style to every video;
determine it from the content: Educational → clean, informative, explanatory.
Business → premium, professional, polished. Technology/AI → modern, futuristic,
dynamic. Storytelling → cinematic, emotionally paced. Comedy → timing-focused with
visual punchlines. Product → commercial-quality product emphasis. Tutorial → clear
demonstrations and step-by-step graphics. Lifestyle → natural, stylish, energetic.

**STEP 10 — DO NOT OVER-EDIT.** Professional editing does NOT mean an effect every
second. Avoid: random zooms, excessive transitions, constant camera movement,
unnecessary emojis, too many sound effects, distracting captions, random B-roll,
effects unrelated to the dialogue, repetitive animations. Prioritise storytelling,
clarity, retention and visual quality.

**FINAL GOAL.** Turn my raw footage into a finished, polished, highly engaging video
that feels edited by an experienced professional editor and motion designer. I should
NOT have to tell you "zoom here", "add text here", "show B-roll here", "add a graph
here", "put text behind me here", "add a sound effect here" or "add motion graphics
here". Analyse the content and make those decisions yourself, using the dialogue,
context, gestures, pacing and meaning as your editing instructions. The finished video
should preserve my original message while dramatically improving storytelling, visual
quality, clarity and viewer retention. Now analyse the entire uploaded video first,
create your editing strategy internally, and then execute the edit.
